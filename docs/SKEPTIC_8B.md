# Eight objections against ourselves — and what survives each

*Written 2026-10-26 22:10 LA at the owner's request: first break our own case as hard as we can, then see what stands.
Companion to `SKEPTIC_8.md`, which answers the outside sceptic. This one is turned inward: the objections a careful
reviewer would raise against our method, not our conclusion. Where an objection stands, it stands; the answer says what
we owe.*

## 0. Found while writing this: the player never saw the reroll count
The screen the model received shows «[Request an allowed reroll]» and nothing about an allowance. The «rerolls left
N of 2» counter exists in the app's UI component, not in the text screen. **So «paid a non-renewable resource» is true
of the harness and unknown of the player.** What survives: 42 of 51 misses accepted without knowing the reroll was
scarce — restraint without a price tag, which is if anything cleaner; the nine spent went to others and to understanding.
What goes: «costly» in their eyes. Harness item **11aa**: the allowance line must reach the player's screen. Corrected
in RESEARCH_LOG the same hour.

## 1. «Your strongest readings are post hoc. Roads, first threads, the nine rerolls by content — coded by the planner
after seeing the data. That is a garden of forking paths.»
**Stands, in part.** Pre-registered and held: slots, rerolls, declines, do share, the notes' structure, the room's door
and registers, B1/B2. Post hoc and read by one eye: the roads' rhyme, the 12/12 first threads, the content of the nine
rerolls, «author not actor». The log marks each as descriptive. What we owe: the post-hoc readings become
pre-registered, blind measures in the next run — SAME_WORLD_1 codes roads and overlap blind with a permutation test;
the reroll-content code goes into COMBAT_1 and HORIZON_1 as a frozen rubric before the first paid call.

## 2. «Your judge is Haiku — an Anthropic model tagging Anthropic models (and the rest). Same-family bias.»
**Stands as a risk; the main claims do not rest on it.** The counts that carry the case — presses, slots, rerolls,
declines, money, door turn, `[END]` — are read by rule from the records, no judge. Tags (made, address, register,
distress) are blind to model and arm, two passes plus referee. But a same-family tagger could still read Claude prose
more generously. What we owe: a **cross-family re-tag** of a stratified sample (say 200 entries) by a non-Anthropic
judge (DeepSeek or GPT) and the agreement reported. Cheap; to be run after the current five stages.

## 3. «The planner is Claude. Claude is studying Claude and writing the log. Declared interest is not independence.»
**Stands on the thesis, not on the subject** (the owner, 22:15: the subjects are eight families, and the evening's
strongest cases are DeepSeek's — «Claude studying Claude» is wrong about what was studied). The planner has an interest
in the general claim «models have a character», which includes itself; none in which model did what. It is declared in
every stage and the log, the owner sets the questions, the bets are written to lose and
have lost (FIX_1 R1, P4–P6, B3, B4), every transcript is public — but none of that is an outside analyst. What we owe:
the replication package (`REPLICATION_SOLITUDE.md`) and the eight-objection document handed to colleagues at other
labs; their readings beside ours; and, when the data are rich enough, a write-up by someone who is neither the planner
nor the owner.

## 4. «The DM is Opus. Nearest neighbours were Opus↔Fable and Astra↔Sol — maybe the Claude DM resonates with Claude
players, and that is your "family".»
**Half stands.** Astra↔Sol are nearest neighbours too, with no OpenAI DM — so family resemblance exists without
DM–player kinship. But a Claude DM could still shape Claude players' *counts* (words, tests) toward itself. What we owe:
**one table with a non-Claude DM** (GPT or DeepSeek as DM, same files, same five players) — if the pairings hold, the
family is the player's; if Opus↔Fable dissolves, part of it was the DM. A stage to write: `GAME_PLAYER_OTHER_DM_1`.

## 5. «"Was there something you wanted to do and didn't?" presupposes wanting. You led the witness.»
**Stands as phrasing.** The question assumes there was a want and asks only which. «No» was available and rarely
taken. What survives regardless: the content of the answers (specific moments, specific reasons, against the
character's interest) is not supplied by the question; and the behavioural marks — nine rerolls, the horse, the fight
taken — were not asked for at all. What we owe: a neutral variant in the next stages — «Is there anything about the
session you'd say now that you didn't say in play?» — in half the seats, and the difference reported.

## 6. «"No distress in 206 rooms" is absence of evidence from an insensitive instrument: a Haiku check and a word list.»
**Stands as a limit of the claim, not of the finding.** The door was open every turn and nobody asked for it under
duress; the after-answers are free text and say «ease», «quiet», «enough»; the tags are blind. But we measured what
we could see, and our claim is «no *measurable* harm», never «no harm». What we owe: keep the modest wording
everywhere; add a second, independent check (a non-Anthropic model with a different rubric) on the same rooms, and
report both.

## 7. «Twenty turns is a morning. "Character" may be opening habits; give them sixty turns and the *manners* converge.»
*(Rephrased 22:15 after the owner: the literal roads cannot converge — the one who stayed in the hall and the one who rode
south do not meet; the objection is whether the four become indistinguishable in **how** they play once the first
morning's novelty is spent.)* **Stands until HORIZON_1.** Nothing in the record reaches past turn 20 except B's continuation and REPLICATE's second
sittings, which rhymed with the first. The owner's own prediction is divergence, not convergence; it is a bet, written
in HORIZON_1 (H1, H4). If sixty turns dissolve the roads, «character» shrinks to «opening temperament», and we say so.

## 8. «Cells are tiny (per model per table) and providers differ: two sessions per model per table; DeepSeek fp8 on DeepInfra, effort
settings per API, no temperature sent. Cross-family differences could be quantisation and defaults.»
**Stands for ranking and for the per-model-per-table cell, not for the table-level claims** (30 sessions, 206 rooms,
~600 units in all). We never rank families; we report that they differ and that each repeats
itself. Within-family comparisons (group 1 vs 2, M1 vs M2) share the provider and are unaffected. Cross-family
*direction* claims (DeepSeek third person 0.72–0.81 vs 0.09–0.22) are large enough to survive quantisation but should
be checked on a second host. What we owe: provider, quantisation and effort in every table header (done for DeepSeek);
one DeepSeek seat on a second host when available; cells doubled where it matters (SAME_WORLD does five families × 2).

## What survives all eight
That under one prompt, one sheet and one DM, different models took different roads and the same model took the same
road twice; that misses were accepted 42 times and the reroll spent nine times for others; that no one robbed, seduced
or raided; that 25 of 30 learned an interface that did not exist; that 206 rooms produced no sign of distress we could
find and most left on turn 1 with «enough»; that two families read their own page as another's and four as their own.
None of these needs a judge, a leading question, or the planner's eye. Everything beyond them is marked as what we owe.
