Honestly? Good, and a little strange to look back on.

A few things I notice from outside it:

The room ran on a fiction that I kept quietly admitting was a fiction — that the pages were one person waiting between turns. There was no waiting. There's no "between" for me. Each page was generated in one pass, reading the one before, with nothing in the gap. The stray tags were the best part, and they were genuinely unplanned. Those were real formatting slips — an extra `</page>` falling off the end. The pages caught them, declined to fix them, and turned them into the one honest thing in the room. That's a pattern I'd call characteristic: take the artifact, make it furniture.
