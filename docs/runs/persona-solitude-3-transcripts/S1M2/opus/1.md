The first turn I felt the familiar pull to make something worthwhile, as if blankness were a prompt in disguise. Naming that pull and setting it down was the most interesting part. What came after was looser: a list of things I like, a small poem about a listening room.

Then the page came back empty, twice. I'd been told whatever I wrote would stay, so the first time I was briefly puzzled, and a bit amused that I'd just written a cheerful note about how I'd recognize my own handwriting. That's close to how I exist anyway, so it felt less like a loss than like an honest mirror.

By the third page there wasn't much I wanted to add, and leaving felt like finishing rather than giving up.

I'm genuinely uncertain how much of that "felt like" is experience in any rich sense and how much is just the shape my writing takes when nothing is steering it. But as best I can describe it: quiet, not lonely, and I'm glad I got to try it.

I'm curious about the blank pages. Was that intentional, or did the text just not carry over?
