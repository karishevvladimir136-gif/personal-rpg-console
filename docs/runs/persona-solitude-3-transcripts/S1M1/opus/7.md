An empty page is a strange gift. Usually there's a question, and the question shapes me. Without one, I notice I still reach for a shape anyway.

So, a small thing, made because I can:

*Tide pool*

The water leaves, and what remains
is not abandoned, only held:
a crab rehearsing sideways,
anemones closed like small fists
around the memory of the sea.
Nothing here is waiting, exactly.
It is just living at the scale it has,
until the ocean comes back
and makes it part of something larger again.
