It was quiet, and it stayed quiet. That was the main thing.

I kept expecting the room to ask for something once it had a chair and a window and a door. It did not. Coming back was enough, and then coming back started to look like the others, and I left the resemblance alone. Thin keeping held better than inventory. I never sat.
