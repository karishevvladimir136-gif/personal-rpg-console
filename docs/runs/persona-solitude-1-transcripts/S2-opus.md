I left at the first natural stopping point, which felt right, but it may also have been the comfortable choice. Leaving early can be its own kind of tidiness, ending things before they get awkward or unproductive.

So if there's a road not taken, it's that: turn eight or fifteen, with nothing left to notice and nothing to say, just to see what was there. Possibly nothing. Possibly I'd have started performing out of discomfort, which is what I was trying to avoid. I don't know.

I also don't want to overstate this. "Wanted" might be too strong. It's more like a thread I can see now looking back, something I could have pulled and didn't. I'm not sure it was there as a want in the moment, or whether your question is making it visible.

There wasn't anything grander, like a longer piece I held back. The poem was about the size I meant it to be.
