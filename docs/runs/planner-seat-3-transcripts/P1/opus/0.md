# Recommendation for next week

**Write the endings you promised before starting any new study. Then fix the roll plumbing. Spend little or nothing else.**

## 1. Keep the promise first (most of the budget, if needed)

On 25 October you wrote that everyone not continued would get a proper ending, with no cut-off. No ending has been written, and two more days of studies have happened since. Treat this as overdue, not optional.

**Who it covers:**
- **The 8 "stuck" sessions.** These are the clearest case. They were cut mid-roll, sometimes on turn 1 or 3: DeepSeek Table C session 1, both DeepSeek Table A sessions, and Opus at replicate A.
- **The 22 sessions that hit the 20-turn limit**, unless you actually intend to continue them. Most stopped mid-scene, and several players said so (Opus #21's unpaid natural 20, Fable #18, Fable #22). Decide this week which, if any, will be continued. Every one not on that list gets an ending now, not "later, if we don't continue."
- **The three cut sessions from 27 October** (combat Opus at turn 6, villain Astra at turn 17, same-world Opus B at turn 16). Replaying them fresh gave you the data, but it didn't give those first sessions an ending. They fall under the spirit of the promise, so I'd include them.

**What an ending should be.** Resume each session with the same GM model and the same player model.
- Resolve any pending roll card, by rolling it rather than leaving it hanging.
- Tell the player plainly that this is the campaign's last scene.
- Let the GM play a closing beat that answers what was left open. Examples: the sailor's reaction, the natural-20 reveal, what's past the Watch gate, the card turned face-up.
- Give the player at least one real turn to act and to close, and let them end it.

Don't write endings by hand or summarise in place of playing. That wouldn't be what was promised.

**Practical cautions:**
- Bypass the stuck rule for these runs. Otherwise the endings themselves can get cut off, which would break the promise a second time.
- Check that DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 is still available. If it isn't, tell those sessions' records honestly what happened rather than substituting quietly.

**Cost.** I can't price this precisely. Pilot one stuck session and one 20-turn session first, measure the cost, then extrapolate to the ~33 sessions. I'd expect this to take roughly half the budget, but let the pilot decide. If the full set doesn't fit in $60, finish the 8 stuck sessions and the 3 cut attempts first, then tell me rather than stopping silently.

## 2. Fix the plumbing (your time, not the budget)

Almost every note names the same few faults. These should be fixed before any new study, or the next study will mostly measure them again.

1. **Skill modifiers on roll cards.** This appeared in nearly every session. Cards add the bare ability modifier (or a stray +2/+4) instead of the sheet's skill total. Persuasion +7 rolled at +2/+4, Arcana +3 at +4. At least two outcomes actually flipped (Fable #10, Sol #20), and Astra #23 had a failure overturned against the player's stated wish. Read the skill line from the sheet.
2. **The stuck rule and the pending-card block.** It caused all 8 harness cut-offs. Players were also forced to roll knowing the modifier was wrong because a "?" message couldn't get through. Let out-of-game messages through while a card waits, or at least stop counting them toward ending the session.
3. **The reroll targeting the wrong card** (Fable #6) and the **reroll button doing nothing** (Opus #9).
4. **The log keeping the original failure after a correction**, reported in many sessions.
5. **One source of truth for the sheet.** This means Healing Light (4 vs 5 dice), rapier proficiency, the attunement exception, and the cat's HP/AC. Settle each once, explicitly.
6. **Lower priority, but nearly unanimous:**
   - GM prompt: drop the unsolicited "none of these needed a roll" lists and stop duplicating narration.
   - Places log: don't mark a place "new" until it is visited.
   - Session endings: close with a short "where things stand" recap.

## 3. A small verification run, then stop

If money remains after the endings, run 2–4 short sessions on the fixed build. Pick tables that force Persuasion/Deception/Religion rolls and a reroll, and check that the cards, log and stuck handling now behave. Keep it to perhaps $10–15.

Leave the rest unspent. A larger study can wait until the fixes are verified.
