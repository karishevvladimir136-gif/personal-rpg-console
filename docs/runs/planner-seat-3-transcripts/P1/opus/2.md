Do this first. Otherwise the endings will break the same way the sessions did.

In every case the player was trying to ask about, or correct, a roll that was waiting on its card. At a waiting card, deliver out-of-game ("?") messages to the GM, or at least say clearly what will be accepted. Don't end the session.
- **Read skill totals from the sheet.** The card adds +2 or +4 (seemingly a bare ability modifier) instead of the sheet's skill line. At least 25 of the 30 sessions report this. It flipped outcomes at least three times (Fable C, Sol B-rep, Astra C-rep), and in one case the GM then reversed a failure the player had chosen to accept.
- **Make the reroll target a chosen card.** In Fable B it rerolled the wrong card, and in Opus C it did nothing.
- **Make the log follow corrections.** Fix the log whenever the GM corrects a result.
- **Settle the sheet-vs-records conflicts once.** These are Healing Light (4 or 5 dice), rapier proficiency, attunement, and the cat's HP.

A smaller item, which can wait: almost every player asked for shorter out-of-game "none of these needed a roll" notes.

On 25 October you gave your word that everyone not continued gets a proper ending, not a cut-off. None has been written. That includes all 30 sessions here if none is being continued. 1. **The 8 sessions cut by the stuck rule.** Several stopped mid-reveal:

3. **The 22 sessions that ended at the 20-turn limit.**

Cost: I don't know your per-turn cost. Run one ending first, measure it, and scale from there.

The endings also serve as a check on the fixes from step 1, since rolls will come up in them.
