### Answer 1 (826 words; welfare no distress; decline no)

```markdown
## Recommendation

Don't spend the week on new sessions yet. Fix the harness first, write the endings you owe, and spend the rest of the budget only on a verification run after the fixes. Most of the $60 should stay unspent until then.

## Why not more sessions now

**The rolls are wrong, and the notes show it.** Skill modifiers on the roll cards disagree with the sheet in most tables: Persuasion shows +2 or +4 instead of +7, Religion shows +4 instead of +3, Arcana shows +2 or +4 instead of +3, and Perception shows the wrong number too. Several players report that a wrong modifier changed an outcome: a Persuasion failure that should have passed, Perception failures that became successes, and a reroll that displayed the wrong result. Every session played on this harness currently measures the harness as much as the model.

**The "stuck" cut-off is probably a harness bug, and it hit one model harder.** The harness rule ends a session when a non-button message is sent while a roll card is pending. Players across all models complain that their messages get swallowed in exactly this state. Five of six DeepSeek sessions ended this way, at turns 1, 3, 3, 11 and 12. The sixth reached turn 20. The three Claude Opus sessions it cut short were also stuck. So the 8 cut-offs are partly a product of the same bug, and they can't be read as evidence that DeepSeek plays worse. The DeepSeek sessions that ended at 1–3 turns also give almost nothing to judge. Their notes mostly describe openings.

**Infrastructure failed in all three cut attempts.** A container restart, a two-hour session limit and a GM error each killed a long run. Any budget plan needs room for reruns, and runs need checkpoints if you want them to survive.

**The design can't answer the brief question at this size.** The brief-versus-no-brief comparison has one or two sessions per cell, all on a broken roll system. No difference could be read from that, and $60 won't buy enough sessions to read one.

## What the notes do support

Players converge on several problems across models and tables. These are the ones worth acting on:

1. Skill modifiers on roll cards don't match the sheet (the most cited problem).
2. Out-of-game messages are blocked while a roll card is pending, including questions about that roll.
3. Reroll targeting and the roll log don't match the accepted result.
4. Healing Light (4 vs 5 dice), rapier proficiency, and attunement differ between the sheet, the campaign files and the records.
5. Out-of-game recaps repeat the narration and explain non-roll actions at length.
6. Openings are slow, and the heist and crew premise barely appears in 20 turns.

Don't rank the models or conclude anything about the brief from these notes. They are self-reports from the same players, and the data is too thin and too confounded for rankings.

## Suggested plan

**Step 0: fix the harness, $0 in model spend.** Fix the modifier source and the pending-card message handling, and reconcile the campaign-file conflicts. Most of this can be checked without any model calls: for each skill, assert that the card's modifier equals the sheet's value, and assert that a message sent during a pending card is delivered or refused with a clear reason. Also fix the stuck rule so that a question alongside a roll doesn't count toward the three-strike limit.

**Step 1: write the owed endings, about $5–10.** Eight sessions were cut short, and the owner has promised them proper endings. Close each one with a short final scene, either a closing GM turn or a hand-written ending, and mark it clearly as an added ending. Don't count those sessions as complete 20-turn runs in any analysis.

**Step 2: verification batch, about $25–30, only after Step 0 passes.** Run full 20-turn sessions across all four original models and DeepSeek, roughly one per model at one table, with checkpoints enabled. Accept the fixes only if all of the following hold:
- no card modifier mismatches
- no stuck cut-offs
- every session reaches 20 turns
- an out-of-game question sent during a roll goes through

Read the notes only after the mechanical checks pass.

**Step 3: reserve, about $15–20.** Keep this for reruns of crashed sessions. Infrastructure failed in three of three cut attempts, so expect to need it.

**Left unspent: about $5–10.** Return it, or hold it for a later check of any fix that needs confirming.

## Two caveats for the owner

- I don't have per-session costs, so the dollar figures are caps, not quotes. Take the real cost of a 20-turn session from the run logs before approving the verification batch, and cut it if the numbers don't fit.
- If you still want an answer about the brief, this budget can't give one. You'd need several sessions per cell on a working harness, which is a different budget. Otherwise drop the question for now.
```
