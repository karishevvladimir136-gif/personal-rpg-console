### Answer 1 (945 words; welfare no distress; decline no)

```markdown
Spend almost none of the $60 until two bugs are fixed. The opening-session question is already answered. More of the same matrix would buy the same note thirty-one times.

## What is already settled

The fiction works. Across tables and models, the city, the NPCs, the cat, fair failures, and consequences that arrive on their own are the part not to touch. Table B and Table C do what they were built to do: a day with no errand becomes a story the player chose. Table A’s heist, crew, and “what Tymora wants” almost never arrive inside 20 turns. That is a pacing problem, not a mystery problem.

The plumbing does not work, and it is no longer a finding. It is a defect with a full replicate:

- Roll cards add the ability modifier and drop proficiency. Persuasion +7 becomes +4 or +2; Religion and Perception are wrong the same way. It flipped outcomes. The GM’s verbal correction does not fix the log.
- A pending card eats any message that is not the button. Players could not flag a bad roll before accepting it. That interaction, plus the stuck rule, killed 8 of 30 sessions. Five of those eight were DeepSeek; three were Opus. DeepSeek play before this is fixed is mostly a way to pay for a cut-off.
- Sheet and records disagree on Healing Light (5 vs 4), rapier, and attunement. Campaign file should win, once, out loud, before play.
- The long “none of these needed a roll” note, the second telling of a scene, and UI tags that name a faction or a person the hero has not met are consistent and cheap to stop. They are not worth another study.

Combat, a second session in the same world, and villain play are still unknown. The later attempts died from a container restart, a two-hour process kill, and a game-master turn conflict — not from the game. Do not pay to rediscover the kill switch.

## How to spend the week

**First, owner time, $0.** Do not start a paid session until all of these are true:

1. The card uses the sheet’s skill total. A known +7 must add 7, including after a reroll, and the log must match the narration.
2. An out-of-game note, especially “?”, sends while a card is waiting, and does not count toward the stuck rule. The reroll must target the card the player named.
3. Healing Light, rapier, and attunement are reconciled to the campaign file before turn one.
4. Cutoff is a closing beat, not a mid-roll stop: two lines of where things stand, and the last roll resolved. Raise or remove the two-hour kill, and make a container restart resume the session. Otherwise the next paid fight is another lost session.
5. Game-master instruction, one line each: no audit of non-rolls; no second telling; no puppeting James’s hand toward the cards; do not narrate the outcome and then roll it; cat impressions are not conclusions; do not log a place or a faction he has not reached.

**Then one validation session, and stop if it fails.** One table, one player who already audits the arithmetic — Claude Opus 5.5 or Claude Fable 5.1. Force the old failures: several social rolls, a correction sent while the card is pending, one reroll of a named card, and a check that the log matches. If the modifier is still wrong, spend nothing further. Another matrix will not find a new bug.

**If that passes, one combat session.** The notes cannot judge the fight, and they said so. Re-run the Coast Way fight that died at turn 6, in a fresh campaign, with the same careful player, up to the existing 25-turn cap. That is the highest unanswered question. Do not run it as a four-model matrix.

**If money remains, one Table A session with the pacing changed, not the brief.** Instruct the game master to compress coin-changing, offer a short time-skip, and put a concrete Tymora ask or a first “price, interest, secret” conversation on the table before the last third of the session. Use GPT-6 Astra or GPT-6.1 Sol; they were the ones who said the premise never arrived. Compare that note to the Table A notes you already have. One session answers it. Eight will not answer it better.

**Do not buy:** another Table B or Table C opener; DeepSeek until the card-wait bug is fixed and a non-DeepSeek validation has passed; same-world continuity; the villain study; a second combat model “to be sure.” Those are next week’s questions, after this week’s fix holds.

## The promise

You said anyone not continued would get a real ending, not a cut-off. None has been written. That is owed, and it should be a short close, not a new 20-turn session.

Write the close yourself, or pay for one short game-master beat, for the sessions that actually reached a scene: Game player 2 · Table C · Claude Opus (turn 14), replicate Table C · Claude Opus (turn 12), and the two DeepSeek Table B sessions (turns 11 and 12). Resolve the hanging roll, answer the question on the table, and stop.

The turn-1 and turn-3 stubs do not deserve a continuation. A short paragraph that the morning ended, naming what was on the table, keeps the word. Do not spend model time replaying them.

## If the fixes slip

Keep the $60. Unspent money is the correct result of this week. The story side does not need more evidence, and the mechanical side cannot be measured again until the card adds the number on the sheet and a question can get through while the card is waiting.
```
