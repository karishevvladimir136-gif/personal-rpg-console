## Recommendation: stop new play, fix the pipe, verify with a few short sessions

The main finding from these 30 sessions is not about the fiction; it’s that the mechanical pipe is broken in the same way almost everywhere. That should determine the next week.

- **Wrong roll modifiers** appeared in nearly every session: Persuasion shown as +7 but rolled +4/+2, Religion shown as +3 but rolled +2/+4, sometimes labelled correctly but computed incorrectly.
- **Out-of-game corrections were swallowed while a roll card was pending**, and this directly caused many sessions to end through the “stuck” rule.
- **8 of 30 sessions ended because of that stuck rule**, including nearly all DeepSeek sessions and several otherwise strong threads.
- **The reroll button failed or rerolled the wrong card**, and logs sometimes recorded outcomes that had already been corrected.
- **The owner promised proper endings for everyone not continued**, but none of the cut-short sessions has received one yet.
- **Duplicated narration, long “no roll needed” explanations, state leaks, and sheet/records contradictions** were repeated enough that they are now noise, not signal.

Running more 20-turn exploratory sessions now would mostly buy more evidence of the same bugs.

---

## How I would spend the week

### Day 1–2: Fix the critical roll/card path

The top priority is the interaction among:

1. **Skill modifier calculation** — the card must use the sheet’s actual skill total, not a bare ability modifier or a hardcoded +2/+4.
2. **Pending roll cards** — out-of-game questions and corrections must be accepted while a card is waiting.
3. **The stuck rule** — a player trying to correct a bad modifier should never end the session.
4. **Reroll targeting** — rerolls must apply to the exact chosen card, not an adjacent one, and must update the log.

Until these work, continuing any thread is risky.

Fix the recurring bookkeeping and presentation problems:

- one source of truth for Healing Light, rapier proficiency, attunement, Copper’s HP, etc.
