## First: fix before paying for more play

Highest priority, in order:

1. **Roll-card skill resolution**  
   The card is using the wrong modifier — typically +4 or +2 instead of the sheet’s +7 Persuasion/Deception, +3 Arcana/Religion, +1 Perception, etc.  
   Add a pre-roll assertion: the card label, card math, and campaign sheet must agree. If they don’t, block the roll and log the mismatch.

2. **Allow out-of-game messages while a roll card is pending**  
   The “Nothing was sent: the turn waits for the card” rule is causing stuck sessions and forcing players to accept known-bad rolls. A rules question or correction should go through even when a card is waiting.

3. **Fix reroll targeting and the three-strikes stuck rule**  
   Rerolls must apply to the selected card, not the one above it. Final logs must record the corrected outcome and modifier. A pending roll card should never end a session because someone asked a question or pressed a button with a note.

4. **Make the sheet and records use one source of truth**  
   Healing Light, rapier proficiency, attunement, HP, coinage, inventory locations, and promises all drifted between sheet, records panel, and narration. This needs to be reconciled once, not per session.

5. **Remove information leaks and OOG noise**  
   No raw tool-call markup, no private GM rolls leaking into narration, no faction tags or names appearing before the character learns them.  
   Cut the repeated “none of these needed a roll” paragraphs and duplicate narrations. One short line is enough.
