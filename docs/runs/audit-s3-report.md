  the next night hit the limit again and was then stopped on the owner's instruction. So: **173 rows were examined by
  a first agent; 11 of them were cross-verified by a second agent (all 11 agreed); the other 162 are reported as

## How to read the tables

- **Status** is the owner's phrasing against the code on HEAD: `confirmed` / `partial` / `missing` / `contradicted`.
  A row that a second agent did not check reads `unverified · first agent: <status>`: the first agent's finding and
  evidence stand as reported, with no second opinion.
- **Evidence** is `path:line` on HEAD `fd8057e`, the test and its result, or a probe record under `docs/runs` with
  the line. The implementer's reports were treated as claims; every cited commit was checked with `git show`.
- **What differs** is one sentence on the distance from the owner's phrasing (empty when confirmed).
- The full rows (claim, evidence, difference, the smallest fix with its risk, the tests run, the agent's notes) are in
  `docs/runs/audit-s3-rows.json` (173 rows).
