He holds the man's eyes a moment, friendly, then lets a phrase fall in passing, light as weather talk but in the cadence of the roofs: *"Chalk's still wet at the gate."* In cant it means *I've seen your mark, and I know whose house this is.* He watches how the man takes it: a flicker, a glance at the post, nothing at all.
