> Beregost wakes to hammers. From every smithy along the street the ringing comes through the shutters of Ostrin Vell's counting-house, where the morning light falls on brass scales and rows of bound ledgers. Sefa Marrin, Pell Dunmore and Bram Halloway stand at the long counter. The three of you met here only this morning, and Vell has just hired you together.
