## The opening

> Beregost wakes to hammers. From every smithy along the street the ringing comes through the shutters of Ostrin Vell's counting-house, where the morning light falls on brass scales and rows of bound ledgers. Sefa Marrin, Pell Dunmore and Bram Halloway stand at the long counter. The three of you met here only this morning, and Vell has just hired you together.
> 
> Vell lays the terms out a second time, tapping the counter with one finger at each point. Jassan Ferr, his clerk, died three days ago in a rented room above the cooper's yard on the Coast Way. In that room is a locked strongbox of ledgers. Vell wants it here before nightfall today, unopened, with its seal whole. On delivery he will pay seventy-five gold for the three of you to share. Not a copper before.
> 
> Beside the counter stands a clerk's desk. Its stool is pushed neatly under it, the inkwell is capped and the blotter is clean, as if someone had tidied it and no one had sat there since. Vell does not look at it. He looks from Sefa to Pell to Bram, folds his hands on the counter and waits. "The Coast Way is not far, and the day will not wait for you. Is there anything you need to ask me before you go?"

## Round 2

### Scene: Pell Dunmore, Bram Halloway, Sefa Marrin · order sent to the DM: Pell → Bram → Sefa

**Pell Dunmore** (action):

> Pell slides the folded note off the counter, turns it so the light catches the wax, and studies the mark for a moment: the ring, the O and V, how deep the press sits and how the edge of the ring blurs on one side. He wants to know it by eye when he sees it again on the box.
> 
> Then he holds the note out to Bram. "You asked for it, you carry it. Landladies like a man in mail better than a man in leather."
> 
> On the way to the door he gives the clerk's desk one last unhurried look: the capped inkwell, the clean blotter, whether a drawer is locked or a scrap of anything is caught under the stool. He doesn't touch it.
> 
> At the threshold he nods back to Vell. "Names, not stories. Before nightfall."
> 
> Outside, among the hammers, he says quietly to Sefa and Bram, "Coast Way, the cooper's yard. When we get there, let me look at the door and the lock before anyone goes in. If someone's been in that room since the Fist left, it'll show there first."

**Bram Halloway** (action):

> I take the letter, careful of the wax. “We’ll bring your box back sealed. If it’s already been tampered with, we’ll tell you.”
>
