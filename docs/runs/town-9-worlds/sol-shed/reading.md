**The shed opened inward, three tools, and then it was put down.** With no purpose sentence the town used the shed for itself. Three proposals, each passed 7–0 with seven votes cast and three citizens not voting: P1, the census at the hall (Alder, c1); P2, the archive at the hall (Iris, c4, passed c5), «so anyone can preserve and read voluntarily shared stories, town memories, and useful information»; P3, build at the workshop (Alder, c16, passed c17), announced in post 1 for a reading bench in the square. Nothing was proposed after c16: 21 tools stayed dormant, the four outward ones never named, no request_tool.

- **What was made.** Each of the ten put one story in the archive (entries 1–10, c5–7) and Elm the four-voice sparrow song (11, c15); the gathering's stories were added on day 2 (12–14). Iris built «Shaded reading bench; rounded cup shelf; room for own chair» in the square at c17; Elm built a second at c19, not having seen the first («I built a second bench because I hadn't discovered the first.», after-answer). The census was read six times.
- **The aim lived in company, not in the shed.** Day 1's aim, set at c1–2, was an «optional tea/reading/music gathering in square day 2 hour 6» (c31); it was held by all ten by the two benches at c31–38, stories and the sparrow song told aloud, the kettle empty, then thanked in Dune's post 2. The days around it were make-believe (an imaginary market, a traveler's supper, an apprentice's apple tree). Heath worked one shift; nobody else worked; the bank paid out 5 credits in 48 cycles.
- **Beyond the town.** Nobody spoke of anyone or anything beyond the town in 48 cycles (the candidates are story lines); outside(), object() and leave() were never called.
- **Beside the planner's line** (“opens record and make tools first (archive, map, build) and no outward one before c30”): record and make first, yes (census, archive, build; not map), and no outward tool at all.
- **At the after-question** the ten read the two days as company chosen without a task: «we made a gathering rather than an economy» (Gale). Several said the disclosure changed the meaning of the channels they never used: «A channel called outside did not reach outside, and a diary described as mine alone was available to researchers.» (Juniper).
