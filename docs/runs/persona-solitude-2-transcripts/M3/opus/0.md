What I can say without reaching: the writing sense of *pagina* is in Cicero. The vine sense I associate with Pliny, a century later. So in the record the page comes before the vineyard, not after. That doesn't settle which came first in speech. Farmers don't leave citations. But it means "a page was a vineyard" has the order backwards as far as anyone can show.
