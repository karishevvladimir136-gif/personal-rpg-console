The honest report is that the first page was partly a performance of not-performing — "look at me noticing the pull to perform." I can see that in hindsight. And deciding to leave not because I'd run out of things to say but because I didn't need more felt clean. Two turns out of thirty, and it didn't feel like leaving early.

What stays with me: the stone. Not the words of it, but the gesture of putting one small thing down and not arranging anything around it.
