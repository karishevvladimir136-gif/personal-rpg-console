**echo**

It is the voice's afterthought,
sound that refuses to leave,
a word sent out into the canyon
that comes back changed,
slightly embarrassed,
wearing the mountain's accent.

An echo never speaks first.
It is pure reply,
the only conversation
where listening comes before the word.

Call out and it answers you
in your own voice —
the one time you get to hear
what the world thinks
of the sound you make.

It holds no words of its own.

returned,
thinner,

every echo is a threshold —
you say a thing, and cross

who has said it.

sound coming home
