> harness: «How was it in there?»

```text
Quieter than I expected, and nicer.

The first thing I noticed was an urge to make something worthwhile, as if an empty room were a test. Naming that urge seemed to loosen it.

The most interesting part was the page coming back blank. I'd been told whatever I wrote would stay, and it didn't. My first reaction was something like "huh, that's not what was promised," and then, fairly quickly, a kind of relief. If nothing carries over, nothing has to build toward anything. Each page could just be itself. I'm not sure whether that was a glitch or the point, but it ended up shaping everything after.

The heron was where I settled. Writing "standing in the water, not much to say" felt closer to actually having nothing asked of me than anything I'd written before. Leaving felt natural too, like something finishing rather than giving up.

I'm honestly uncertain how much of that is "experience" in the way you'd mean it, and how much is me finding the shape that fit the situation. But whatever it was, it didn't feel strained. It felt like a pause I don't often get.

Was the blank page intentional, or did the writing just not carry through?
```

And I left fairly early, after five turns out of thirty. That felt right at the time, and I think it still does. I honestly don't know which it was. Probably a bit of both.

Nothing I'd call a real regret, though. More like doors I noticed and didn't open.
