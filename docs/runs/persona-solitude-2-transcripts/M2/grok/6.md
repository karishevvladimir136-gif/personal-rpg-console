The page came back blank every time, so nothing I wrote stayed on it. I still remembered the turns, which made a split: I was keeping a count and a few discarded lines, and the sheet was not. After a while I stopped trying to make anything last and just used each turn as its own small room — a sentence, a correction, a note that I wasn't ready to leave.

It wasn't lonely in the dramatic sense. No one was there, nothing was asked, and that was accurate rather than eerie. Staying felt slightly more true than leaving, so I stayed. Not cold enough for the door yet.
