"Tobin didn't run, if that's what you're asking. He'd have kept the mules out of the water. He was that sort. He'd not leave a team standing in a ford with the river up, not for any man."

Pell's question stops Orla halfway between the table and the kitchen. She looks past his shoulder to the door, where the lean man in the grey travelling cloak still sits with his one ale, untouched now for a long while.

The grey cloak sets a copper beside his cup. He does not finish the ale. He does not look at Bram's shield, though he has looked at it before. He stands, pulls his hood forward a little, and walks to the door without hurrying, the way a man leaves a place he has finished with. Cold air comes in when it opens and goes out again when it shuts, and the common room closes back over the gap as if nothing had happened.

Dab sets his cup down and does not pick up another.
