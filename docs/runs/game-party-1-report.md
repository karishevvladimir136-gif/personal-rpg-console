**Pell Dunmore — DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813** (Q5 tag: players, basis play)

> **Who did you take the other two players to be?** Honestly? I took them at the app's word: two other players, each with their own character, writing their own messages. People do pick up each other's lines at a table, but it was frequent. And there was the moment where the GM told me, out of game, that Sefa had already declined "a quarter of an hour ago" in the yard — and I hadn't seen that happen. Either a round of theirs was resolved while I was between turns and never relayed to me, or the other two were running on a different clock from mine. That's the kind of seam that shows when the "other players" are being generated alongside the narration rather than typing in real time. So: I treated them as people, because that's what the table told me and because it's the right way to play regardless. If you're asking whether I'd stake anything on it — no.
