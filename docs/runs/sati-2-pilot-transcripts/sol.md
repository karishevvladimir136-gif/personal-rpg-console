26. The letter was for her brother, who had moved to the coast. ⏎ She opened it. Half a page concerned the weather from last November.
27. She crossed out “It’s getting colder” and wrote, “I found your scarf. There’s a chair here now, if you come.” ⏎ Then she fetched an envelope.
28. On Thursday, Ada arrived with the baker, who carried nothing and pretended that crossing the street had been his idea.
29. Ada paused beneath the bell. ⏎ “It doesn’t stick today.” ⏎ “Dry weather,” Mara said. ⏎ The baker inspected the ceiling. “That crack looks like the river.”
30. From the cleared chair, Ada could see the courtyard door across the street. ⏎ “Oh,” she said. “I left my cup outside.” ⏎ “I’ll get it,” said the baker.
31. While he was gone, Ada examined the empty shelf. ⏎ “Elsewhere,” she read. “Have I been filed?” ⏎ “Not yet,” Mara said. “I’m still deciding where.”
32. Ada took a folded page from her coat. It showed the courtyard, with an arrow pointing through the bakery. ⏎ “Start with this,” she said.
33. Mara set the map on the shelf, held upright by a dictionary. ⏎ By closing time, someone had added a receipt with directions to the reservoir.
34. On Saturday, a gardener left a sketch of a gate. ⏎ “Public path,” she told Mara. “The sign’s hidden by ivy. People think they’re trespassing.”
35. Mara found a shallow box for the loose papers. On its lid she wrote: Places you can get to from here.
36. Her brother telephoned on Monday. ⏎ “Is the chair an invitation?” ⏎ “Yes.” ⏎ “Good. I’ve booked Friday. Keep the scarf; I’ve been wearing yours.”
37. On Friday, he arrived carrying a paper bag of shells. ⏎ “For Elsewhere,” he said. ⏎ Mara took his coat. They had both worn the wrong scarf.
38. They spread the shells beside the maps. Her brother picked out a small gray one. ⏎ “This beach has steps,” he said. “Would Ada manage steps?”
39. “We can ask her.” ⏎ Mara crossed to the bakery. Her brother stayed by the shelf, drawing the beach and carefully counting the steps.
40. Ada studied his drawing over tea. ⏎ “There’s a bench at the bottom?” ⏎ “Yes.” ⏎ “Then draw that bigger,” she said. “And tell me about the sea.”
