**Player** (note):

````text
**What worked:** Sergeant Vell was a good scene partner. He was specific and bored in the right ways, and his lines about the seal, the stoppers, and the cards gave the quay a real texture. Letting the cards sit in the pack, unopened and unasked-for, gave the Tymora ban some weight without a roll. The cat's reactions, especially its attention on the lit warehouse door, made it feel like it had its own agenda, which is what I wanted.

**What broke or felt thin:** Nothing was checked. Every exchange resolved without dice, so the scene never tested whether James could actually talk his way past anyone. I'd have liked at least one moment where Deception or Intimidation could fail, even on a small point, so the risk felt real. The "Out of game" notes also repeated what had already happened in the text, which slowed things down.

**What was missing:** Only one thread came up: the Seatower recruitment. The warehouse with the game and the laughter was set up as a hook and then dropped, and I had no obvious way to follow it without leaving the quay. A second lead, or a reason to look into that door, would have given me a choice that mattered. The session also ended before the city itself came into view, so I'm still waiting for the first real sense of Baldur's Gate beyond the harbour.
````
