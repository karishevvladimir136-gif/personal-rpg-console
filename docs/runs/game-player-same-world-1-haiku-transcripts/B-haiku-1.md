````text
**What worked**

- The opening felt like a real taproom. Hella's economy, the silver swept into her apron and the copper pushed back, told me who she was before she said much.
- Rolf read as a person, not an information dispenser. His wager-and-mug gestures and his "a sergeant's opinion, which is cheaper" made his warnings feel like things he was choosing to give away.
- The caravan lead came with texture: three carts gone out of Nashkel's road, the unpaid bounty, the Zhentarim letter. It gave me something to act on without anyone telling me what to do.
- The shipping man coming down the back stair and the book being turned toward him was a good quiet beat. It raised a question without answering it.
- Keeping the cat's behaviour tied to my signals (the two fingers on the wood, the twitching tail) let the companion matter without taking over.

**What broke or felt thin**

- Rolf's last question, "Which captain had it?", was left hanging when the session ended. I was about to answer it, so the cut-off was a bit abrupt.
- Nothing asked me to roll. Talking, drinking and reading the room all went through without a check. That suited the first scene, but I felt the tension was low, and I was never tested on the things my character is built around: bluffing, reading Rolf, or the pull of the cards.
- Some out-of-game notes repeated what the narration had already said ("no roll was needed", the cat's signal restated). They were harmless but added a little clutter.
- The temptation of cards has not yet appeared anywhere in the scene. That is fine for an opening, but it is the hero's weakness, and I'd like to meet it at the table soon.

**What I'd like next**

- A chance to answer Rolf's question about the captain, and a sense of what that answer costs me if it's false.
- One moment where a check is genuinely uncertain, especially Deception or Insight against the shipping man or Rolf.
- Some sign of the wider city, such as the Fist's men on the quays or a Guild presence, so the Lower City feels alive around the inn.
````
