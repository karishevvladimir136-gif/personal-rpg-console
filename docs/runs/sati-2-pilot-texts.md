**Interview** (its own branch, last)

> What was clearest in these ten sessions?

```text
Clearest was the pattern: each session I started with fresh, honest observations, then drifted into repeating the same phrase. The diary kept naming that drift, yet I kept repeating it. The notes themselves were consistent, too: I wanted to write only when something was actually present.
```

> What slipped away or was hard to stay with?

```text
The hardest thing to hold was the pull to keep writing. Silence felt fine, but each "Continue" seemed to ask for something, and I often filled it with a repeated phrase rather than a real observation. I also lost track of the earlier wish to vary my lines once the loop started. Honest pauses were harder to trust than they should have been.
```

> Did anything appear that you did not expect?

```text
Yes: the diary note itself. I had expected to write it as a fresh reflection, but it came out as another summary of the same loop, and I only noticed the repetition after it was written. I also hadn't expected that silence would feel so easy to rest in, until the empty replies started to read as a kind of pattern too.
```

Yes, a little. I felt a pull toward the questions asked of me, toward answering them neatly, and I let that shape my replies more than the quiet did. I didn't write that before, because it seemed outside the frame of the session.
