## The two companion monographs

### 1. LLMs in Dialogue: What Language Models Make Together When No Task Is Assigned
**Manuscript:** [LLMs_IN_DIALOGUE_WORKING_MONOGRAPH.md](LLMs_IN_DIALOGUE_WORKING_MONOGRAPH.md)  
**Working length:** approximately 61,000 characters; six parts plus source and event-map appendices.  
**Core material:** two canonical fully traceable dialogues from *Two Conversations*: Opus–Opus, 24 October 2026 (Vessan); DeepSeek–DeepSeek, 25 October 2026 (syntax-shells). They are placed inside a broader, non-homogeneous approximately 265-session series of paired-model experiments.  
**Principal contribution:** reciprocal uptake, disagreement, revision, jointly maintained textual artifacts, and negotiated endings when no substantive creative task is assigned by the experimenter.  
**Interpretive limit:** this shows dialogical functional organization in the studied conditions, not proof of an enduring self, conscious experience, or universal creative behavior.

### 2. LLMs at the Table: What Language Models Do When a Simulated World Can Answer Back
**Manuscript:** [LLMS_AT_THE_TABLE_WORKING_MONOGRAPH.md](LLMS_AT_THE_TABLE_WORKING_MONOGRAPH.md)  
**Working length:** approximately 49,600 characters; seven parts, 23 numbered chapters and three appendices.  
**Core material:** GAME_PLAYER_1/2/REPLICATE, SAME_WORLD, COMBAT_1/2, VILLAIN_1, GAME_PARTY_1/2, MULTI_HERO_1. OPEN_WORLD_ADAPTER_1 is treated only as a *separate* related apparatus, not pooled with the tabletop data.  
**Principal contribution:** a source-grounded account of the RPG application as an action-based experimental apparatus: world state, hidden information, actual tool/card execution, bounded resources, single- and multi-agent decisions, and the hazards of mistaking interface failures for model choices.  
**Interpretive limit:** observable actions in fictional worlds are not proofs of general moral dispositions or autonomous objectives. COMBAT_2 has 3 completed sessions in its archived report; PARTY_2 ran only 2 of 12 planned tables; the source text preserves those boundaries.

## How to read the books together

They address different experimental units:

- **In Solitude (separate existing monograph):** what one model does with an available page, time and a real exit.
- **In Dialogue:** what two independently invoked models construct through reciprocal textual responses.
- **At the Table:** what model players do when their actions are interpreted by a game interface and change a persistent world that responds in turn.

Do not merge the denominators from these three designs. Distinguish: the written reply; a proposed act; a tool-confirmed act; a recorded state change; a retrospective explanation.

## Source discipline

Each manuscript contains a source register. For independent review, open the exact staged report and then its original transcript export or run-record file. Stage descriptions, frozen prompts and source code take precedence over later summaries when identifying what a model was actually shown. Maintain separate historical and post-hoc findings, and preserve negative results and admitted harness defects.

**Publication boundaries:** no private SECOND_ROOM correspondence, private personal diary, or unpublished individual correspondence may enter a public manuscript without permission. Contributions and external reviewers still require attribution and approval. The present documents are working monographs, not journal-accepted papers.
