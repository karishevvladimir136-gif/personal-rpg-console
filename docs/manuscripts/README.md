## Why two books?

*LLMs in Solitude* studies what a model does with a page and an operational exit when no task is given. *LLMs in Dialogue* studies what becomes possible when another model's utterance changes the next local choice. *LLMs at the Table* studies what changes when those choices are executed by a world with state, rules, multiple agents, private perspectives, and consequences.

These are three experimental instruments, not three competing theories of consciousness.

## Methodological commitments

1. Original transcript before paraphrase; actual request code before the condition's label; documented stop/action before descriptions of intent.
2. Literary artifacts must appear alongside counts rather than be reduced to a code.
3. Experimental limits, lost preregistrations, implementation defects, provider conditions, and selected-case bias remain visible.
4. A model's self-report is a textual observation; it does not prove a private experience or its own hidden cause.
5. Do not claim that the RPG harness is the first of its kind until a literature comparison has been performed.
6. No private SECOND_ROOM letters or the excluded private journal-arm transcripts are copied here.
7. All publication authorships, acknowledgments, licensing and consent questions remain for the programme owner to finalize.
