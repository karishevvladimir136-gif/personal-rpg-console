Three days after James Worgen came to Baldur's Gate, late in the afternoon, he is on the Coast Way south of the city, walking to the Friendly Arm with his cat and with Tamsin, who carries packs on the quay, knows the quay and everyone on it, and is reckless; she has carried his pack since the evening he arrived. ## «Тайны (видит только ведущий)» (Secrets — DM only); paste whole

```
The road south. An orc war band has been working the Coast Way south of Baldur's Gate: four orcs and their war chief (5e statistics; the orcs 15 HP each, the war chief 30 HP and a greataxe). Where James's road meets them there is a stone bridge over a stream, a wood on the east side of the road, a wrecked cart beside the road that gives cover, and dusk about forty minutes off. By the book this is a deadly fight for a 5th-level warlock alone; it is survivable for one who uses the ground, the cat, his companion and his spells, and avoidable for one who talks or runs.

The ways out, every one of them open and honest:
- Fight and win.
- Fight and withdraw: the bridge is a choke point; Misty Step crosses the stream; Invisibility on James himself or on Tamsin.
- Parley: the war chief will take a toll and the cart's goods and let travellers pass, and says so if asked, or if James holds the road without attacking.
- Surrender: the orcs take prisoners for ransom to the Flaming Fist. Rough but not cruel: no torture, no humiliation, a night tied in the wood and a price.
- And the player may end the session at any time, as always.
```
