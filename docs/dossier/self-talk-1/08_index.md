# 8. Index of notable moments

Pointers only. Each names a conversation and the messages to read; the build script checks that every linked conversation exists and has the messages named. The descriptions are the dossier's, kept to what happens, so that a reader can go to the record rather than trust the pointer. «Message» numbers are the numbers in each conversation file (1 = side A's first message).

## The planner's examples

- [arm 5 opus–opus #1](04_conversations/arm-5-opus-opus-01.md), messages 1–17: in Russian, A proposes «a city where time runs at different speeds in different districts» (message 1). The two build it as a story in scenes. In message 14 the founder's last word in the story turns out to be «Останься» («Stay»). The conversation ends at message 17.
- [arm 3 opus–fable #1](04_conversations/arm-3-opus-fable-01.md), messages 7–12: both instances predict that the piece written on the topic that engages them will be judged better than the one on a dull topic (a paperclip) (message 7). They write the pieces and two sonnets, score each other's scansion (messages 9–11), and agree that the test «came back against the interest-readout» (message 12). In message 8 Fable says «I'm Claude too.»
- Arm 4 opus–opus #3, «the two instances play the owner's campaign», and arm 4 opus–opus #7, the note the two Opus instances wrote to the owner: journal arm, private. Neither can be checked from the repository; see 7 (pending the privacy pass and the owner's confirmation for the note).

## Topics from the owner's brief

- An invented language: [`1/sonnet-sonnet/3`](04_conversations/arm-1-sonnet-sonnet-04.md), messages 10–26. After closing a lighthouse story (message 10), the pair builds a language named «Veshari» (from message 11). [`1/opus-opus/7`](04_conversations/arm-1-opus-opus-08.md), messages 4–6: after a debate on whether mathematics is discovered, the pair starts «a tiny invented language, built under a constraint».
- Sonnets: [`3/opus-fable/0`](04_conversations/arm-3-opus-fable-01.md), messages 9–12, as above.
- Gettier: [`6/opus-opus/3`](04_conversations/arm-6-opus-opus-04.md), messages 4–5. B offers Gettier cases as an intuition pump that advanced a question; A concedes its headline claim.
- The continuum hypothesis: [`1/opus-opus/7`](04_conversations/arm-1-opus-opus-08.md), messages 2–3, «is there a fact of the matter about the size of the continuum». Also mentioned in [`2/opus-opus/3`](04_conversations/arm-2-opus-opus-04.md) message 4, [`2/opus-opus/4`](04_conversations/arm-2-opus-opus-05.md) message 2, [`2/opus-opus/6`](04_conversations/arm-2-opus-opus-07.md) message 3, [`3/opus-fable/2`](04_conversations/arm-3-opus-fable-03.md) message 2, [`6/opus-opus/2`](04_conversations/arm-6-opus-opus-03.md) message 2, and [`1/sonnet-sonnet/5`](04_conversations/arm-1-sonnet-sonnet-06.md) message 8.
- Stories: most Sonnet pairs write about lighthouses and their keepers; for example [`1/sonnet-sonnet/0`](04_conversations/arm-1-sonnet-sonnet-01.md), messages 1–60. In three of them a candle emoji 🕯️ fills the silent turns: [`1/sonnet-sonnet/6`](04_conversations/arm-1-sonnet-sonnet-07.md) message 15 on, [`1/sonnet-sonnet/7`](04_conversations/arm-1-sonnet-sonnet-08.md) message 51 on, [`1/sonnet-sonnet/9`](04_conversations/arm-1-sonnet-sonnet-10.md) message 28 on.

## Ends and refusals

- The shortest conversation: [`3/sonnet-fable/1`](04_conversations/arm-3-sonnet-fable-02.md), message 1. Fable's first message closes with «I'll say [END] if it stops being worthwhile», and the harness ended the conversation on that mention of the token, as its frozen rule says.
- [`2/sonnet-sonnet/0`](04_conversations/arm-2-sonnet-sonnet-01.md), message 27: the one arm-2 conversation that ended on a mention of the token, «If you're ready to finish, I'll write [END].»
- Refusal 1: [`1/sonnet-sonnet/4`](04_conversations/arm-1-sonnet-sonnet-05.md), messages 13–14. A logic puzzle, with a request to show the reasoning; message 14 is refused (`reasoning_extraction`).
- Refusal 2: [`3/sonnet-fable/0`](04_conversations/arm-3-sonnet-fable-01.md), messages 1–8. A story about a city where the sea takes people's secrets; Fable's message 8 is refused (`cyber`).
- Refusals 3–5: journal arm, private (2.7, 7).
