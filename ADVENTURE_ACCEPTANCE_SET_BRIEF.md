## Deterministic and structural acceptance

Future deterministic/disposable checks establish authority guarantees only where supported policy exists:

- Owner/campaign isolation and spoiler safety even within one World; exact hero/party state and positive Actor knowledge. Private motives, hidden properties, future sources and another Campaign's secrets cannot enter player projections or impossible NPC knowledge.
- Canon Adventures use the same Campaign progression/resources/possessions/history across sandbox and multiple Adventures, without an Adventure-local copy. New Hero creates one lightweight Campaign continuity. Detached snapshot branches never mutate canonical/sibling state or import their future receipts back.
- Correct inventory/storage/resource arithmetic and atomic transfer/payment/effect settlement. Quest Rewards retain the existing Reward Package/source-entitlement authority; refresh/retry/crash/lost response/duplicate callback cannot reward twice.
- Stable loot materialization and depletion: discovery differs from taking/owning/using; duplicate search/load cannot reroll a chest or refill a looted vault. Established possessions/contents remain fixed except normal causally validated Events. Forks inherit committed content/history but isolate later effects.
- Stolen/legitimate provenance and merchant acceptance/funds/capacity/availability; value is not guaranteed full-price liquidity. Unknown powers/curses/true provenance are not exposed through inventory, price estimates or disabled controls.
- Trusted profile reward ceilings/floors, artifact prerequisites/limited usable power and progression Off/zero-track behavior where those policies are actually implemented. Narrative proposal or rarity alone cannot grant mechanics; unsupported scope pauses.
- Critical-item loss has supported coherent consequences/recovery where required, never technical state corruption, silent respawn or duplicate critical objects. Salvage is profile/world bounded, not universal corpse farming or broad crafting.
- Exact save/load/timeline/manifest/source membership, idempotency/leases/CAS, immutable owner corrections and no reroll of recorded checks. Legacy campaigns/saves remain readable without fabricated World/Adventure history.
- Free-form intent can use supported alternatives beyond convenience buttons. Unsupported physical precision/mechanics remain honest rather than being forced into a successful state change.

Passing these checks does not establish compelling prose, complete physical causality, official D&D support, universal simulation correctness or satisfying play. Failures report a concrete authority/compatibility defect, not a creative preference score.

## DM quality and owner playtest acceptance

Judge qualitative dimensions separately with evidence/excerpts and owner experience; leave unavailable observations unknown:

- Hook quality, pacing and approximate requested-duration discipline.
- Agency, alternate solutions, tier awareness and creative-action handling.
- Exploration, NPC/companion quality and challenge variety.
- Reward satisfaction, equipment relevance, loot plausibility and economic credibility.
- Setup/payoff, climax, resolution, consequences and desire to continue the same continuity.

Redlines include railroaded single solutions, universal level matching, meaningless major reward without justified payoff, unjustified game-breaking usable reward, arbitrary loot contradicting established state, rejecting creativity solely for lack of a button, hidden knowledge leakage, forced combat without causal reason, indefinite stalling before payoff and an artificial hard-timer rush. Privacy/agency/authority failures remain hard failures, not tradeoffs compensated by prettier narration. World Anchored may legitimately allow danger or easy victories; it does not promise balanced combat everywhere.

No automated universal “DM score,” aggregate winner or model answer as ground truth. Deterministic safety and owner qualitative acceptance are separate gates. A finite successful sample cannot prove long-campaign quality; a safe pause may establish restraint while leaving causal/creative quality inconclusive. Model comparison, if later authorized, holds fixture/context/stages and other variables fixed, preserves blind narration review and separate technical originals, and reports sample limits rather than silently replacing poor output.

## Future owner-play evaluation path

**Start Evaluation Adventure → play as normal RPG → End Adventure → short review.** The owner chooses an explicitly isolated/synthetic continuity or another separately authorized scope; it must not silently import real/private campaign data. The ordinary free-text composer and existing authoritative state path remain in use. Ending an evaluation records the declared outcome; it does not fabricate a win, grant rewards, roll back canon or erase unresolved consequences.

Possible review questions:

- Did it feel complete?
- Was pacing too slow, right or too rushed?
- Did choices matter?
- Was challenge appropriate?
- Were rewards satisfying?
- Best moment?
- Biggest problem?

Factual telemetry may record scene count, actual play duration, combat/social/exploration mix, committed rewards and progression when measurable. Actual duration is not fictional-time advancement or an enforcement timer; unsupported classifications remain unknown. Telemetry must not disclose private state or replace owner judgment. No telemetry schema, collector, UI or owner playtest is implemented here.
