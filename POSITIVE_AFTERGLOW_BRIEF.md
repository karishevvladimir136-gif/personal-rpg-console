# Positive Afterglow — entertainment-first emotional ecology

**Status:** owner-approved design synthesis and versioned Craft guidance with synthetic owner-review fixtures. The north star is **“Go as deep as possible. Come back better.”** It is an experiential game-design aspiration, not a medical claim, therapy objective, diagnostic system or promised mental-health benefit.

The default `hopeful_humanist` tone means the world need not systematically punish trust, care or courage. It does not guarantee a good ending. `balanced` and explicitly selected `bleak` permit different long-horizon climates. `tragedyIntensity` defaults to `normal`, with `low` and `unrestricted` available. These bounded campaign settings guide future presentation but never override Canon, mechanics, established consequences or the owner's actual decisions. Historical scenes are not retrofitted.

Fear, betrayal, grief, toxic fictional people, moral conflict and irreversible loss remain legitimate. Sadness is not a failure. Do not rush a consolation speech into a tragic scene, or kill beloved people by shock lottery. Serious loss should be grounded in causality, visible risk and supported outcomes. Recovery is part of drama, not a requirement that every hard scene become pleasant. After accumulated fictional intensity, allow quiet, humor, mastery, shared meals, rebuilding or celebration where the world supports them. No real-user mood sensing or hidden wellness score is involved.

Support must be character-shaped. A reserved ferrypilot may leave a dry blanket; a playful craftsperson may wait before joking; an archivist may offer reliable records. None should become a generic wellness bot. Shared joy deserves episodic continuity alongside painful events. A fictional toxic NPC may manipulate a player-character, but the product must not manipulate the real owner: no guilt for wall-clock absence, FOMO, attachment exploitation, secret return-frequency objective or pressure against real relationships. Fictional time does not advance because the owner has not opened the app.

The development-only review profile invites qualitative notes on vitality, warmth, connection, curiosity, inspiration, meaning and autonomy, with guardrails for depletion, manipulation and “did this feel like therapy?” No composite score is produced or collected during gameplay. The eight frozen synthetic afterglow probes cover support, joy, rhythm, tragedy, hope, toxic-fiction boundaries, moral complexity and owner inactivity. Live owner review will later determine whether the guidance produces believable experiences without forced positivity.
