# Cognitive DM Craft — bounded Mega DM v1 synthesis

**Status:** owner-approved product synthesis, implemented as versioned DM Craft in this candidate. This is not a validated psychology formula, a new simulation authority, or a claim of live model quality. The accepted state, Canon, Memory, rules, World Events, saves and controlled-player agency remain higher authority.

The desired cognitive experience is a world that can surprise the player while remaining learnable. Player sovereignty means any plausible free-form intent can be considered; world sovereignty means intent does not dictate outcome. A validated action may leave physical, social, informational or institutional fingerprints. Failure can change the situation without forcing a reset. The DM should offer credible uncertainty: hypotheses can improve from evidence, but neither a railroad nor arbitrary twists deserve trust. Strong surprises are coherent in retrospect.

Narration should supply details that help the player imagine, infer and act. It need not exhaustively describe what a renderer would show. Social consequences propagate only through witnesses, institutions, rumors and relationships with a plausible route. A universal omniscient reputation is an anti-pattern. Player-created goals, routines, places and objects may become durable world content when supported by the existing authority; they are not subordinate to a model-generated main quest.

Long campaigns accumulate narrative capital. An old object can matter because of its provenance; an old place can matter because it changed; a departed person can remain meaningful through absence. Use Return With Delta when source-linked change is available. Limit the number of active unresolved concerns while allowing dormant Threads to return when relevant. A session may land in relief, joy, silence or anticipation without a manufactured cliffhanger.

**Implementation boundary:** `core-doctrine@2` is a concise guidance version. The `cognitive-world@1` card is eligible through the existing deterministic public-context selector. At most two relevant Cards enter each stage, and a pinned turn keeps its exact versions on retry. No Craft text directly mutates state, promotes Canon or authorizes a consequence. The frozen 48-scene living-character fixture adds 13 cognitive probes; deterministic tests check source links, ordering, privacy and selection artifacts. Whether generated play feels independently real remains a separate future human/model-quality question.

**Project heuristics, not established laws:** mix uncertainty with learnable evidence; use quiet space after intensity; make familiar content more valuable through accumulated history. These are design judgments to evaluate against owner experience, not psychological or medical claims.
